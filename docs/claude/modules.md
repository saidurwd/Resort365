# Module notes

Moved from CLAUDE.md (the per-module part of its Tenancy section). Read the module's part before working in it; the tenancy rules themselves stay in CLAUDE.md.

- **Cottages and rooms** (Property module, Setup menu): cottage types, room types, cottages (`booking_mode` `rooms_only` / `whole_only` / `both`) and rooms are property-level and soft-deleted; amenities and departments are tenant-wide. These screens work on the **current property**. New cottages come from the quick "add cottage with N rooms" form (`CreateCottageWithRooms`, numbers from `RoomNumberSequence`). Capacity comes from `OccupancyCalculator`. Deleting a room asks `Modules\Property\Contracts\RoomUsage::hasFutureBookings()`; Property binds a "no bookings" default until Reservation binds the real one (Step 1.6). Permissions: `property.{cottage,room,amenity,department}.{view,manage}`.
- **Guests** (Guest module, sidebar **Guests**): guests, companies and travel agents are tenant-wide. Other modules use `Modules\Guest\Contracts\GuestLookup` (search, find, isBlacklisted, companies, travel agents) and listen to `GuestsMerged` (move records from the merged guest to the kept one). `SaveGuest` normalises the phone (`PhoneNumber`, setting `guest.default_calling_code`) and keeps `id_number_hash` (`IdNumberHasher`) in step with the encrypted `id_number`; never write guests around it. Search (`GuestSearch`) uses indexed prefixes: phone, email, ID hash, name words. ID numbers show in full and ID documents download only with `guest.guest.view-id`. The navbar **quick search** (`$quickSearch`, shared by the Guest module) opens the guest list with the term; reservations join it later.
- **Rates** (Rates module, sidebar **Rates**): seasons (with periods), rate plans, rates per room/cottage type × season × weekday set, date prices (`rate_overrides`) and restrictions, all per property. Rates reaches Property only through `Modules\Property\Contracts\InventoryCatalog` (room and cottage types as `UnitTypeSummary`, keys like `room_type:4`). `NightlyRateResolver` (pure) decides a night's price; `RateCalendar` loads a plan's data for a date range. Rates factories find a property through `ResolvesProperty`, because they may not use Property's models. Deposit and cancellation policies (one default each per property; a rate plan may choose others) and promotions also live here; `DepositCalculator`, `CancellationFeeCalculator` and `PromotionMatcher` are pure and unit-tested (the §6.5 worked example is a test).
- **Reservations** (Reservation module, sidebar **Reservations**): `inventory_locks` (one row per room and night, `UNIQUE(room_id, stay_date)`; types reservation, hold, out of order, owner block) is the single source of availability. `AvailabilityService` / `PricingService` use only contracts: `InventoryCatalog::rooms()/cottages()` and `Rates\Contracts\RateLookup`. The pure `AvailabilityCalculator`, `RestrictionChecker` and `PriceCalculator` hold the rules and are unit-tested. Reservation binds `LockedRoomUsage` as Property's `RoomUsage`. **Bookings:** `CreateReservation` (with `BookingQuoter` for prices and the deposit) writes reservations, items, nightly snapshots, guests and locks in one transaction; a lock clash throws `RoomNoLongerAvailable`. The booking wizard (`/reservation/bookings/new`, session state in `BookingWizard`) uses Guest's `GuestRegistry` for new guests and Rates' `RateLookup` for deposits. The concurrency test runs separate PHP processes (`Modules/Reservation/tests/Support/book.php`). **Tape chart** (`/reservation/tape-chart`): `TapeChart` loads a property's rooms and locks for 14 or 30 days, and the pure `TapeChartBuilder` turns locks into bars. Dragging a room booking posts to `tape-chart/move` (`MoveOnChart`: `MoveRoom` in house, `ChangeItemRoom` before arrival). The Alpine component is `resources/js/ui/tape-chart.js`, and bars are placed with the `tc-col-N` / `tc-span-N` classes from `_tape-chart.scss`.
  - **Managing bookings** (`/reservation/bookings`, list and a page with tabs Summary, Rooms, Guests, Payments, History): `ModifyReservation` (edit → review → save; replaces items, nights and locks in one transaction through `ReservationWriter`, a clash keeps the original), `ChangeDeposit` (0% waives; outside the policy needs `reservation.deposit.override`), `CancelReservation` (fee from `RateLookup::cancellationQuote`), `AddReservationGuest` / `RemoveReservationGuest` / `MakePrimaryGuest`. Every change is written to `reservation_logs` by `ReservationLogger`. `ReservationBalance` (pure) holds the deposit, balance and payment-status rules. `reservations.no_room_charges` (booking page switch, `SetRoomCharges`) stops outlet charges; `ReservationLookup::mealEntitlement` gives meal-plan covers per period (pure `MealEntitlements`). A tentative booking is confirmed by `DepositConfirmation` once paid ≥ deposit (`ReservationConfirmed`); cancelling fires `ReservationCancelled`.
  - **Hold expiry:** `ExpireTentativeHolds` runs every five minutes in the scheduler (locally: `php artisan schedule:work`, or once with `php artisan reservation:expire-holds`).
  - **Quotes** (`/reservation/quotes`): saved from the wizard's last step (`SaveQuote`, prices frozen in `quote_items` / `quote_item_nights`), emailed (`SendQuote`), declined, or booked at the quoted prices (`ConvertQuote` → `CreateReservation::handle($data, $quoted)` with a `BookingQuote` rebuilt by `QuoteSnapshot`).
  - **Guest emails:** `SendBookingNotifications` listens to `ReservationCreated` / `Confirmed` / `Cancelled` and queues `SendGuestEmail` (tenant-aware); `GuestEmailComposer` fills the template (`GuestEmail` enum = template keys), attaches the voucher or quotation PDF (`BookingDocuments`) and logs it in the booking history. Emails need a queue worker locally (`php artisan queue:work` or `composer dev`) and land in `storage/logs/laravel.log`. Demo seeding discards them. Settings: `reservation.payment_instructions`, `reservation.quote_valid_days`. Voucher: `/reservation/bookings/{id}/voucher` (confirmed bookings). Report: `/reservation/reports/sources`.
  - **For other modules:** `ReservationLookup` (a `ReservationSummary`), `ReservationTabs` (add a tab to the reservation page; Billing adds Payments), and `BookedRatePlanUsage` bound as Rates' `RatePlanUsage` (plans used by upcoming bookings cannot be deleted). Reservation listens to Billing's `PaymentReceived` (`ApplyPayment` sets the total paid) and Guest's `GuestsMerged`. Listeners are registered in the module's `EventServiceProvider::$listen` (discovery is off).
- **Front office** (FrontOffice module, sidebar **Front Office → Front Desk**, `/frontoffice`): the front-desk dashboard of the current property on its business date, check-in at `/frontoffice/check-in/{reservation}` (ID via `GuestRegistry::recordIdentity`, same-type room change via `StayOperations::changeRoom`, Billing's payment form with `return_to` and a security deposit, `CheckInGuest` → `StayOperations::checkIn` → `GuestCheckedIn`), a printable registration card and a **Front desk** tab on the booking page. Check-out at `/frontoffice/check-out/{reservation}` (departures list, booking's Front desk tab): `PostStayCharges` (Reservation's `StayOperations::unpostedNights` → Billing's `FolioSettlement::postRoomNights` → `markNightsPosted`), settle folios with Billing's forms (payments with `folio_id`, `To city ledger`, refunds), then `CheckOutGuest` (`StayOperations::checkOut` + `FolioSettlement::invoiceAndClose`, then `GuestCheckedOut`). FrontOffice depends on Billing's contracts; Billing never on FrontOffice. Other modules add night-audit blockers through `Modules\FrontOffice\Contracts\NightAuditBlockers` (Restaurant: open POS sessions). Stay changes (Step 2.4) at `/frontoffice/stay/{reservation}` (in-house list, Front desk tab): move a room (`StayOperations::moveRoom`, rate kept unless `frontoffice.stay.reprice`), extend, leave early (`extendStay`, `shortenStay`; `StayRepricer` keeps nights and totals in step). Check-in takes `item_id` to check in one room of a group; `ReservationSummary` has `groupName`, `itemsTotal`, `itemsCheckedIn`. Groups: `reservations.group_name` (wizard pricing step), rooming list `SaveRoomingList`, and Billing opens a master folio with room charges routed to it. Walk-ins: `/reservation/bookings/new?walk_in=1`. FrontOffice reaches bookings only through Reservation's contracts (route parameters are ids, not models). Permissions `frontoffice.desk.view`, `frontoffice.checkin.perform`. **Night audit** (Step 2.6, `/frontoffice/night-audit`, `RunNightAudit`): departures still in house block it; it posts in-house nights up to the business date (`StayOperations::unpostedNights($id, $upTo)` → `FolioSettlement::postRoomNights`; not in `at_checkout` mode), marks no-shows (`StayOperations::markNoShow`), releases expired holds (`expireHolds`), snapshots `daily_statistics` (`ReservationLookup::occupancy`, Billing's `DailyTakings`, the pure `DailyStatsCalculator`) and moves the date on with Property's `BusinessDates::advance` (only from the expected date). `night_audits` is unique per property and date. Scheduled by `RunDueNightAudits` (pure `NightAuditSchedule`; setting `frontoffice.auto_night_audit`; locally `php artisan frontoffice:night-audit --now`). Flash report: `/frontoffice/reports/flash?date=`. Permissions `frontoffice.audit.{view,run}`, `frontoffice.report.view`.
- **Housekeeping** (Step 2.7, sidebar **Housekeeping**): room status board (`/housekeeping/board`; cleaning status through Property's `RoomStatuses` + `room_status_logs` via `RoomStatusChanger`; occupancy from `ReservationLookup::roomOccupancy`, never stored), cleaning tasks (`CreateTask` is idempotent per room, date and type; `PrepareRoomsAfterDeparture` on `GuestCheckedOut` and Reservation's `RoomMoved`; `CreateDailyTasks` on `NightAuditCompleted` and `housekeeping:daily-tasks`; `ProgressTask` steps start/finish/pass/fail/skip from the pure `TaskTransitions`), OOO/OOS blocks (`BlockRoom` → Reservation's `RoomBlocks::block()`, which refuses booked nights with `RoomBlockRefused`; `EndBlock` releases from the business date), work orders (`ReportWorkOrder`, `UpdateWorkOrder`), preventive schedules (`CreateDueWorkOrders`, pure `PreventiveCalendar`, `housekeeping:preventive` daily) and lost & found. Permissions `housekeeping.{board.view,room.update,task.manage,task.perform,block.manage,work-order.create,work-order.view,work-order.manage,schedule.manage,lost-found.view,lost-found.manage}`; there is no default attendant role (give a custom role `housekeeping.task.perform`).
- **Restaurant** (Step 3.1, sidebar **Restaurant**; setup in the back office, service on the POS): outlets per property (`SaveOutlet`; one default tax category, `bill_prefix`, opening hours), printers (`SavePrinter`; browser printing in v1), kitchen stations (`SaveStation`; display, printer or both, a printed station needs a KOT printer), POS terminals (`RegisterTerminal` returns the plain device token once, stores its SHA-256), outlet access (`outlet_user`, `SyncOutletAccess`, `OutletAccess::outletIds()`; `restaurant.outlet.access-all` = every outlet), dining areas and tables with the SVG floor-plan editor (`floorPlan` Alpine component in `resources/js/ui/floor-plan.js`, `SaveFloorPlan` + pure `FloorPlanGeometry`, canvas 1000 × 600, grid 10). Permissions `restaurant.outlet.{view,manage,access-all}`, `restaurant.floor-plan.manage`, `restaurant.access.manage`. Menu (Step 3.2): categories (`SaveMenuCategory`), items (`SaveMenuItem` with variants by name, modifier groups, combo components; names are json per menu language, `HasTranslations::translated()`), modifier groups (`SaveModifierGroup`), outlet price lists (`SavePriceList`, rows sent as `rows_json`; price, station of that outlet, schedules, 86, meal-plan eligible; `CopyPriceList`; `MarkSoldOut`), per-outlet schedules (`SaveMenuSchedule`, pure `MenuAvailability`) and CSV import (`ImportMenu` + pure `MenuCsv`, all or nothing). Setting `restaurant.menu_languages`. Permissions `restaurant.menu.{view,manage,mark-sold-out}`, `restaurant.price.manage`. POS (Step 3.3, `/pos`, layout `<x-layouts::pos>`): devices register with a terminal token (`PosDevice`, cookie `pos_device`; middleware `pos.terminal` sets `PosContext`); staff sign in by PIN (`PosStaff`, `CheckPosPin`, IAM's `PosPins`; middleware `pos.staff` with auto-lock); `pos_sessions` (`OpenPosSession`, `ClosePosSession`, X/Z report on 80 mm paper); manager PIN approvals (`ManagerApprovals::approve()` / `consume()`, add new actions to `ManagerApprovals::ACTIONS`). Settings `restaurant.pos_auto_lock_minutes`, `restaurant.session_variance_limit`. Permissions `restaurant.pos.use`, `restaurant.session.{manage,view,approve-variance}`. `App\Support\Cash\CashCount` is the shared cash-count math. Orders (Step 3.4, `/pos/floor` → `/pos/orders/{id}`, Alpine `posOrder` in `resources/js/ui/pos.js`; JSON under `/pos/api/orders/{id}`, each answering the whole order from `OrderPresenter`): `OpenOrder` (dine-in at a free table, or takeaway), `AddOrderLine` (server price via `MenuCatalog::priceNow`, 86 and schedules, pure `ModifierRules`; open items need `restaurant.order.open-item`), `ChangePendingLine`, `SendOrder` (pure `KotGrouper`, one KOT per station; `course` fires a held course), `VoidOrderLine` (sent lines only; `restaurant.order.void` or a manager approval `order.void-line`; void KOT, wastage), `MoveOrder` (transfer, merge, cancel). Order and KOT numbers per outlet and business date come from `PosNumbers` (inside the transaction; since Step 7.5 they count on from counters on the locked outlet row, `outlets.numbers_date` / `last_order_no` / `last_kot_no` / `last_bill_sequence`, never by locking other rows). KOTs print at `/pos/kots/{id}/print` (stations with a printer, hidden print frame). `PosOrderPolicy` (order.take + outlet access) and the terminal's outlet guard every endpoint. Permissions `restaurant.order.{take,void,open-item}`. Real time and kitchen display (Step 3.5): Laravel Reverb + Echo; private channels from `RestaurantChannels` (`tenant.{t}.outlet.{o}` for POS staff, `tenant.{t}.kitchen.{s}` for the station's display or a person with `restaurant.kds.use`), authorized at the tenant's `/broadcasting/auth`. Broadcast events (`KotSent`, `KotItemVoided`, `KotItemStatusChanged`, `TableStatusChanged` from `OrderTotals::refresh` and table moves, `MenuAvailabilityChanged` from `MarkSoldOut` / `SavePriceList`) carry ids; screens reload JSON (`/kds/api/board`, `/pos/api/floor`, `/pos/api/menu`, `/pos/api/ready`) and poll every 5 s without WebSockets (`live()` in `resources/js/ui/live.js`). KDS at `/kds` (`<x-layouts::kds>`, Alpine `kdsBoard`): a station's display registers with its display token (`RegisterStationDisplay`, cookie `kds_device`, request guard `kds`) or a signed-in person picks a station (`/kds/stations`); taps go through `ProgressKot` (pure `KotTransitions`: start, ready, bump = served, recall). Settings `restaurant.kds_warn_minutes`, `restaurant.kds_late_minutes`. Permission `restaurant.kds.use`. POS and KDS keep their own colour mode per device (`<body data-theme-key>`). Bills (Step 3.6, `/pos/orders/{id}/bill`, Alpine `posBill` in `resources/js/ui/pos-bill.js`): `DiscountOrder` (item or bill, before printing; limits per role from `discount_limits` via IAM's `RoleDirectory`, above them `bill.discount` approval), `OrderBilling` (per-line taxes through `TaxEngine`; service charge = tax code `restaurant.service_charge_code`) and the pure `BillSplitter` / `Allocation` / `DiscountAllocator` (bills always add up; property tests in `BillSplitterTest`), `PrintBill` (numbers `MR-B000001` from `PosNumbers::nextBill`), `ReopenOrder`, `TakePayment` (`PaymentMethod::tenders()`; needs the terminal's open session), `CompBill`, `VoidBill` (same business date, refunds as negative payments), `BillSettlement` (settles bill and order, `RestaurantBillSettled`; `RestaurantBillVoided`). Refusals a manager's PIN can lift carry `PosNotAllowed::$approval`, and the POS asks for the PIN and repeats the step. `SessionCash` sums cash and takings by method. Permissions `restaurant.bill.{settle,reopen,comp,void}`, `restaurant.discount.approve`, `restaurant.discount-limit.manage`. Charge to room and meal plans (Step 3.7): `TakePayment` posts room charges synchronously through Billing's `FolioPostingContract::postCharge` (preset `taxLines` from the bill via `TaxShare`, `outletCharge`, `pos_bill` reference, charge code `restaurant.room_charge_code`) and city-ledger payments through Billing's `CityLedgerAccounts`; stays come from `FolioPostingContract::chargeableStays`; `VoidBill` reverses them (`reverseCharge`, `CityLedgerAccounts::cancel`). Signatures are stored with `TenantStorage`. `RedeemMealPlan` uses Reservation's `ReservationLookup::mealEntitlement` (via `MealPlans`), zero-prices the order's meal-plan items (`pos_order_lines.package_redemption_id`, `OrderBilling`) and records `package_redemptions`; over the plan needs `restaurant.package.override` or approval `package.over`. Restaurant registers `OpenSessionsBlocker` with FrontOffice's `NightAuditBlockers` and the `pos_bill` link with Billing's `FolioReferenceLinks`; the back-office receipt is `/restaurant/bills/{id}/receipt`. Settings `restaurant.room_charge_code`, `restaurant.breakfast_until`, `restaurant.lunch_until`. Permissions `restaurant.package.{redeem,override}`. Room service, reservations and reports (Step 3.8): `OpenOrder` takes an `OrderDestination` for `room_service` (an in-house guest), `location_delivery` (a place) and `staff_meal` (a name; `restaurant.order.staff-meal`; settled as complimentary without a PIN); delivery orders have a `DeliveryStatus` moved by the kitchen (`ProgressKot`) and `AdvanceDelivery` (`DeliveryStatusChanged`). Table reservations: `SaveTableReservation` / `SeatTableReservation` / `CloseTableReservation`, pure `TableAvailability`, `TableReservations` (the floor's *reserved* tiles and list), screen Restaurant → Table reservations, completed by `BillSettlement` (`restaurant.reservation.{view,manage}`). Reports at Restaurant → Reports (`RestaurantReports` with the pure `SalesReport` and `MealPlanReport`; CSV with `?export=csv`; `restaurant.report.view`). The night audit snapshots meal-plan entitlements (`SnapshotMealEntitlements` on `NightAuditCompleted`; Reservation's `ReservationLookup::mealEntitlements`).
- **Accounting** (Step 4.1, sidebar **Accounting**, `/accounting`): the chart of accounts (`accounts`, `SaveAccount` / `DeleteAccount`; group accounts are not postable; `system_key` names the accounts later steps post to; `ChartOfAccountsTemplate` ≈ 90 USALI-style accounts, `SeedChartOfAccounts` is idempotent and runs for new tenants through `SeedChartForNewTenant` on `TenantCreated`; existing tenants: `php artisan accounting:seed-chart [--tenant=slug]`), fiscal years of 12 monthly periods (`CreateFiscalYear`, `ChangePeriodStatus`: close in order and without drafts; reopening needs `accounting.period.reopen` and a reason) and journal entries (`SaveJournalDraft` via `JournalWriter`, `PostJournalEntry`, `ReverseJournalEntry`, `DiscardJournalDraft`; pure `JournalInvariants`: two or more lines, one side per line, debits = credits). A posted entry and its lines are immutable (the models refuse changes); a correction is a reversal. Numbers `JV-…` come from Core's `journal` document type inside the posting transaction; automatic entries will carry a unique `(source_type, source_id, source_event)` (Step 4.2). Lines have optional dimensions (property, department through Property's `DepartmentDirectory`, party guest/company/travel agent through `GuestLookup`; vendors and employees `TODO(step-5/6)`). Permissions `accounting.account.{view,manage}`, `accounting.period.{view,manage,reopen}`, `accounting.journal.{view,create,post,reverse}`; settings `accounting.base_currency`, `accounting.fiscal_year_start_month`. Demo: `DemoAccounting.php` (opening balances, an accrual with its reversal, one draft, the early months closed). **Automatic posting** (Step 4.2): `PostingService` turns operational events into balanced, idempotent entries (unique `source_type`/`source_id`/`source_event`); `PostLedgerEntries` listens to Billing's `PaymentReceived`, `RefundIssued`, `InvoiceIssued`, `CityLedgerTransferred`, `FolioChargeVoided`, FrontOffice's `NightAuditCompleted` (a property day's folio charges: Dr guest ledger / Cr revenue by charge code, service charge and VAT payable; meal part to food revenue) and Reservation's `ReservationCancelled` (fee). It reads facts through Billing's `LedgerFacts` and Reservation's `ReservationLookup::cancellation(s)`, never their models. A closed period moves the posting to the next open one; a refused posting is reported, not thrown (the action succeeds). Accounts come from `AccountResolver`: the tenant's mapping (Accounting → Account mapping, `account_mappings`, keys `guest_ledger`…, `method:cash`, `charge:SPA`) or the chart's system key. Charges flagged `revenue_posted_by_source` are skipped. `php artisan accounting:backpost [--tenant=]` posts earlier events (`BackpostHistory`, safe to repeat); the demo seeder runs it. The journal list filters manual vs automatic. **Restaurant and vouchers** (Step 4.3): `RestaurantBillSettled` posts the bill (`PostingService::restaurantBill`, pure `BillEntryBuilder`; tenders, food/beverage revenue per outlet, service charge, taxes, tips; room-charge bills debit the guest ledger and are skipped by the night sweep), `RestaurantBillVoided` reverses it, and the new `PosSessionClosed` posts cash short/over. Restaurant exposes facts through `RestaurantFacts` (Accounting never reads its models); menu categories carry `revenue_class` (food/beverage, null follows the parent); outlets are mapped as `outlet:<id>:food|beverage`. Vouchers: Accounting → Vouchers (`CreateVoucher`, `VoidVoucher`, `Voucher` is property-level with attachments, numbers `IV-`/`EV-`; permissions `accounting.voucher.{view,create,void}`). **Cash and bank** (Step 4.4): `BankAccount` ties a name to a postable asset account (`SaveBankAccount`; kind `bank` or `cash`); `CreateTransfer` / `VoidTransfer` (`FundTransfer`, `TR-`); statements come in through `ImportStatement` (CSV via pure `StatementCsv`, all or nothing, duplicates skipped by fingerprint), lines are matched one to one to ledger lines of the same signed amount (`MatchLines`, `UnmatchLines`, `AcceptSuggestions` with pure `StatementMatcher`, window setting `accounting.reconcile_date_window`) and `CompleteReconciliation` locks them once every line is matched and the pure `ReconciliationCalculator` difference is zero (`ReconciliationView` feeds the screen and the report, CSV with `?export=csv`). Vouchers may carry a cheque (`cheque_no`, `cheque_status` pending/cleared/bounced; `ChequeTracker` clears it on a match, `MarkChequeBounced` voids the voucher). Permissions `accounting.bank.{view,manage,reconcile}`. Demo: two bank accounts, a transfer, a pending cheque and a reconciled City Bank statement. **Financial reports** (Step 4.5, Accounting → Financial reports): `ReportController` serves `/accounting/reports/{trial-balance|ledger|profit-loss|balance-sheet|cash-flow|departmental|aging-receivable|aging-payable}` with `from`/`to`/`as_of`, `property` (an id or `none`), and `export=xlsx|csv|pdf` (`StatementExport`; permission `accounting.report.export`). `LedgerQuery` sums the ledger, pure builders in `Services/Statements` shape it, `ReportService` returns a `Report` DTO whose figures link to the ledger (drill-down), a ledger line to its entry, and `SourceDocuments` lists an entry's source documents (receipt, invoice, bill, voucher, booking…) on the entry page. Cash flow is indirect (cash = 11xx; investing 15xx; financing 25xx and 3xxx). New posting rules for another module: add a method to `PostingService` and a listener method. **Inventory against the ledger** (Step 5.8, Accounting → Financial reports → `inventory`): ledger balance of each `inventory_<class>` account against `StockFacts::valuation` (stock plus transfers in transit); per-movement cent rounding can leave a difference of a few cents.
- **Inventory** (Step 5.2, sidebar **Inventory**; the catalogue only, stock movements come in 5.3): units (`SaveUnit`, `SeedStandardUnits` for new tenants and `php artisan inventory:seed-units`; kinds count, weight, volume, length, and *pack* units such as box or sack that are converted per item), item categories (tree, `SaveCategory`), items shared by the tenant (`SaveItem`: SKU generated `ITM-00001` if blank, base/purchase/issue units that must be convertible, the item's own conversions and its par and reorder levels per store), stores per property tied to a department (`SaveStore`; kinds main, kitchen, bar, housekeeping, maintenance) and CSV import (`ImportItems`, pure `ItemCsv`, all or nothing, updates by SKU). Quantities have four decimals. Other modules use `ItemCatalog` (find, search, `toBaseQuantity` through the pure `UnitConverter`) and `StoreDirectory`. Restaurant outlets have a `store_id` (their ingredients' store, validated through `StoreDirectory`). Permissions `inventory.item.{view,manage,import}`, `inventory.catalog.manage`, `inventory.store.{view,manage}`. **Stock ledger** (Step 5.3): `StockMovementContract::record(NewStockMovement)` is the one way to change stock (Restaurant, Housekeeping and Procurement use it): an append-only `stock_movements` ledger (immutable models; correct with `ReverseMovement` or `AdjustStock`) and `stock_balances` (quantity and weighted-average cost) locked and updated in the same transaction; costing is the pure `StockCostCalculator`. Quantities are in the item's base unit (pass `unitId` to use another unit of the item), an out-movement leaves at the average cost, a receipt or opening needs its cost, and an `idempotencyKey` makes a repeated call return the first movement. Negative stock is refused (`InsufficientStock`) unless `inventory.allow_negative_stock`. `RecordOpeningBalance` (once per item and store), `AdjustStock`, two-step `DispatchTransfer` / `ReceiveTransfer` / `CancelTransfer` (within a property, `TRF-…`), and `StockBelowReorderLevel` after commit. Screens: Stock on hand, Movements, Transfers, Adjust and open stock. Permissions `inventory.stock.{view,adjust}`, `inventory.transfer.manage`. Demo: `DemoInventory.php` (five stores at Rodela, about 150 items, outlets linked, opening balances, a dearer delivery, transfers, damage). **Requisitions, issues and counts** (Step 5.4): `SubmitRequisition` values the lines at the store's average cost and sends them to the `store_requisition` approval workflow (step *Department manager*, `inventory.requisition.approve`, every amount; `RequisitionApprovalHandler` approves or rejects the document), `IssueRequisition` (all or fewer than asked; then `issued`), `WithdrawRequisition`; `IssueStock` is a direct issue (reason required, `inventory.issue.create`); both go through `StockIssuer` / `StockIssuing::issue(NewStockIssue)` (one `ISS-…` document, every line at the average cost or none, `stock_movements.department_id`, `StockIssued` after commit) which Housekeeping uses for **My tasks → Issue amenities** (the property's housekeeping store, its department). Counts: `StartStockCount` (one open count per store; snapshots each item's quantity and cost; optional category; *blind* hides the expected quantity), `SaveCountEntries`, `CompleteStockCount` (adjusts by counted − expected, where `CountVariance` allows for movements since the snapshot, reason `count_variance`, `StockAdjusted` after commit), `CancelStockCount`; printable sheet at `/inventory/counts/{id}/sheet`. Categories carry an `account_class` (food, beverage, housekeeping, maintenance, operating) that tells Accounting which accounts to use. Accounting reads issues and adjustments through `StockFacts` (Dr the class's cost account with the department / Cr its inventory account; count loss Dr `inventory_shrinkage`, gain the reverse; mapping keys `stock:expense:{class}`, `stock:inventory:{class}`, `stock:shrinkage`).
